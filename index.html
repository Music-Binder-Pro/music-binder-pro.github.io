Music Binder Pro

Organize your music sheets and play with confidence. Music Binder Pro is the essential organizer and player for musicians on stage. Support for PDF and ChordPro files, setlists, visual metronome, and hands-free controls—built for live performance on macOS.

Seamless Sheet Organization

Sort songs instantly by title or artist. Use tabs for setlists and collections. Add notes, transpose ChordPro files, and edit chords on the fly with the embedded editor.

Live Performance Ready

Record time markers for page turns, messages, notes, and now MIDI events. Visual metronome syncs across all screens. Send messages and notes to any display for flawless coordination.

Hands-Free Control

Customize gestures, keys, and shortcuts—no mouse needed. Bluetooth foot controllers like AirTurn BT-105 and external keyboards supported. Keypad keys trigger MIDI events.

Version 3.18 Enhancements

Optimized for macOS 26/Tahoe. New MIDI cues from library, last played dates for screensets, song counts per tab, compact ChordPro rendering, and fixed Tap Tempo hotkey.
